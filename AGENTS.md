# AGENTS.md

Luv in Space is a showcase for [luv](https://github.com/mbrock/luv), Mikael Brockman's Common Lisp GPU workshop: a hard sci-fi spaceflight game in near-future, realistic technology, centered on piloting a rugged DIY spaceship under Newtonian physics from low Earth orbit out into the solar system. It is the prelude to a space adventure with a linear quest line. Built so far: the design (`milestone 1`), the flight core (`milestone 2`): the Mule flown by hand in a 400 km polar orbit under RK4 with J2, the Moon and the Sun, time warp, the outside view, the map, the minimal readout, the crash and restart, and headless flight scripts with screenshots; and the cockpit and the autopilot (`milestone 3`): the DIY cockpit with its screens, buttons, lamps and grapple camera, the autopilot's orbit manoeuvres and rendezvous presets, the five Earth-orbit places, grappling, berthing and towing, and the flight scripts and live runs that show them. Next: the solar system (`milestone 4`).

## Work: `pm`
- Milestones, plans, tasks, decisions: all in `tasks.sql`, only through `pm`. Read `pm instructions` first.
- Start: `pm status`, `pm show <task>`.
- Before a choice: `pm decision list --answered --grep <word>`; answers are binding.
- Human decisions are the exception: decide yourself and record it (`pm decision record`); the developer overrides by superseding.
  - `pm decision add` only for what no agent can decide or find out: accounts, money, licences, publishing, or a contradiction in a milestone.
  - Creative choices (story, names, look, features) and technical choices are the agents' own: record them.
- Follow-up work: `pm task add`.
- Cite milestones, plans, tasks, decisions by id and subject, in backticks: `decision 14: Units and time epoch`.

## Outside pm workers
- Skip this section if `PM_TASK` is set (you are a pm worker).
- Milestones are the developer's; edit them with pm when we answer decisions: `pm decision list`, `pm milestone set <id> --body - <<'EOF'`, then `pm decision answer <id> …`.
- Editing a milestone makes pm queue a revision of its plans and tasks; editing the milestone is usually enough.

## luv
- `../luv` (sibling of this repo): a read-only reference checkout of github.com/mbrock/luv. Read its `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, `wiki/` and code; never change or commit there.
- This repo builds against luv pinned as a flake input, not against `../luv`.
- Something luv lacks: build it here, in this repo; list what would better live in luv in `docs/luv-wishlist.md`.

## Rules
- Push directly to `main`. No feature branches or pull requests.
- Before every commit: `nix build` and `scripts/test` pass; with changes to the simulation, the autopilot or `flights.lisp`, `scripts/test-slow` too.
- Use comments sparingly and keep them short.
- Style of `.md` files: bullet points, concise. Exception: an intro paragraph in full sentences at the top of the document and under each top-level heading.
- Stop background processes by PID (`kill $!`), never `pkill -f`: the pattern can match your own `claude` process.
- Close every game window you open; a worker leaves no process running.
- Unfinished game parts: `PLACEHOLDER(<task id>)` in code and, where visible, on screen.

## Commands
- Dev shell: `direnv allow` or `nix develop`; SBCL with luv on `CL_SOURCE_REGISTRY`, Vulkan/SDL3, `pm`, `git`. The scripts below enter it themselves.
  - `scripts/test`, `scripts/test-slow`, `scripts/screenshot`, `scripts/fly` and `./sly` always re-enter it, so a stale direnv shell or a foreign `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` (`GLIBC_2.43 not found`, `Unable to load … libSDL3.so`) doesn't reach them; run `sbcl` by hand only in a fresh `nix develop` (or after `direnv reload`).
- Build: `nix build` (`result/bin/luv-in-space`); ~17 s from a fresh clone, luv and its dependencies from luv's binary cache; ~14 s when only the game's image builds.
- Run: `nix run` opens the window in the cockpit (V to the outside view), its title listing the main keys; M opens the map (a click on a marker targets it, a double click focuses it, Tab cycles ship, Earth and target; wheel and Page keys zoom, Home re-frames); V toggles the outside and cockpit views (the cockpit as built so far: the scene from the pilot's eye, head look by right-drag, the wheel zooms or, over COMMS, scrolls its log, Home recentres; a left click presses the buttons, MSTR, an AUTOPILOT menu row or the COMMS face, and the hovered button's key shows on the strip), G arms or stows the grapple or releases a load or berth, B toggles refuelling berthed at a depot, Y marks the SHIP log read, ] and [ cycle the target and \ clears it (paused too), O opens the autopilot's presets (in the outside and map views as the autopilot panel top right; the orbit rows, then "RDV <PLACE> <h> H" for the five places and "RDV TARGET <h> H" with a target not a place; Up/Down select, Left/Right change the value (a rendezvous's limit 12–240 h), Return plans and a second Return engages, Backspace disengages, drops the plan or closes; a flight key while engaged gives control back), 7 8 9 0 hold the nose at, away from, along or against the velocity relative to the target ("NO TARGET" without one); after a crash the LOST panel shows, Return restarts from the latest checkpoint (else the start) and N from the start; Escape, Control+Q or closing the window quits. An agent stops it by PID.
- Test: `scripts/test` (Parachute, `luv-in-space/tests`; renders on a hidden canvas, lavapipe without a display; exit 1 on failure; ~44 s measured at the check of `milestone 3`, ~75 s on a fresh clone's first run, which compiles). `nix flake check` runs it in the sandbox (~95 s with the slow check).
- Slow tests: `scripts/test-slow` (Parachute, `luv-in-space/slow-tests`, `tests/slow-flights.lisp`): milestone 3's flight scripts (the approach, rendezvous and preset scripts), each flown twice bit for bit, a line each (game time, steps, wall time, PASSED or FAILED), and plan 27's long runs (searches, plans, rendezvous end to end, full-length station-keeping); exit 1 on failure; ~89 s. Run it before committing changes to the simulation, the autopilot or `flights.lisp`. `nix flake check` runs it as its second check (`checks.<system>.slow`, ~140 s alone).
- Screenshot: `scripts/screenshot out.png [--frames N] [--view outside|cockpit|map]`: a 1280x800 PNG of the start state from the outside view (default), the cockpit or the map with the readout top left (`capture-scene`), from a hidden canvas, headless too; ~3 s.
- Fly a script: `scripts/fly <name> <out-dir>` flies `flights/<name>.sexp` headless (`run-flight`), prints the record (a line per screenshot, expectation, burn and failure, then `END PASSED` or `END FAILED`) and the PNG paths, and writes `<out-dir>/<nn>-<label>.png` (1280x800, `nn` from 01) per `:screenshot` from a hidden canvas, headless too (`fly-main`, `fly-screenshots`); exit 0, 1 on a failure, a failed expectation or a missing script, 2 on bad arguments; ~7 s for `raise-orbit`'s five PNGs, ~10–18 s for the milestone 3 scripts. A script may start from a stated state, `(:start :state (…))` (an orbit, a date, berthed, grappled, …; `docs/architecture.md` "Start states"), which `scripts/fly` and live `fly` start from alike.
- Earth's night lights and clouds: `scripts/convert-earth [out-dir]` downloads Black Marble 2016 (3 km, 8 MB) and the Blue Marble clouds (36 MB; from the Internet Archive when NASA's host does not answer; needs network), checks pinned SHA-256s and writes `assets/earth/black-marble-5400.jpg` and `clouds-5400.jpg` (ImageMagick from the dev shell); two runs identical; ~7 min measured, nearly all of it the download from the archive (the conversion itself ~20 s).
- Star table: `scripts/convert-stars [out]` downloads HYG (needs network), checks its pinned SHA-256 and writes the stars ≤ 6.5 mag; without an argument it rewrites the committed `assets/stars/hyg-6.5.sexp`; ~4 s.
- Live image: `./sly start` (a Slynk image with `luv-in-space` loaded; ~5 s cold), `./sly play` (Escape, Control+Q or the close button end it as `./sly stop-playing` does), `./sly status`, `./sly screenshot /tmp/x.png` (window size, after `./sly play`), `./sly eval '(defun …)' --package LUV-IN-SPACE` (redefine while playing), `./sly failures`, `./sly resume`, `./sly stop-playing`, `./sly stop`; `./sly --help` lists all. Enters the dev shell itself; options go after the code.
  - Flight scripts live: `./sly eval '(fly "raise-orbit")' --package LUV-IN-SPACE` restarts the game at the script's start and flies it frame by frame; `(flight-status)` returns the record lines so far, printed to `./sly log` at the end; `(flight-stop)` ends it. With `:shots "<dir>"` (`(fly "approach-k-17" :shots "/tmp/live-k17")`) the clock pauses at each `:screenshot` step, the window's PNG is written as `<dir>/<nn>-<label>.png` and the flight goes on (P is ignored meanwhile); `(flight-status)` then ends with the PNG paths, which also go to `./sly log` after the record; `approach-k-17` takes ~1,000 s of wall time live (its approach is at ×1); each `preset-<place>` takes ~430–470 s live (measured at the check of `milestone 3`), its record lines equal `scripts/fly`'s. While a flight runs, the flight keys, warp keys, Return and N are ignored; P, C, Home, M, Tab, Page keys, the mouse and quit act.
  - Live, `raise-orbit` takes ~3 s (its burns fly at ×100); to screenshot a moment, pause in the same eval: `(progn (fly "raise-orbit") (let ((t0 (sim-t (game-sim *game*)))) (loop until (> (- (sim-t (game-sim *game*)) t0) 15) do (sleep 0.002))) (setf (clock-paused (game-clock *game*)) t))`, then `./sly screenshot`; `(setf (clock-paused (game-clock *game*)) nil)` goes on.
  - `./sly` is luv's client from the flake input with `play`, `screenshot`, `stop-playing` and status redefined for this game (`scripts/sly-client.lisp`); `sly-server.lisp` boots the image. State in `build/` (~410 MB, git-ignored).
  - Shaders are live: a `define-shader` re-evaluated with `./sly eval '(define-frame-shader earth-fragment …)' --package LUV-IN-SPACE.SHADERS` shows on the next frame (luv live shader pipelines, refreshed in `draw`). A source that fails to parse is rejected by the eval; one that fails to build keeps the last good pipeline, and `./sly status` names it with its error (`Live artifact earth FAILED …`) until a fix rebuilds it; in Lisp `(luv-in-space:report-failed-live-artifacts)`.

## Layout
- `flake.nix`: luv as a pinned input, the package (`nix build`), `apps.default` (`nix run`), the dev shell, the check (`nix flake check`).
- `luv-in-space.asd`: systems `luv-in-space` (`src/`), `luv-in-space/program` (the executable), `luv-in-space/tests` (`tests/`) and `luv-in-space/slow-tests` (`tests/slow-flights.lisp`, run by `scripts/test-slow`).
- `src/`: `package.lisp`, `shaders.lisp`, `main.lisp` (the game: sim, clock, camera, the frame, the death record and glow, `game-restart!`) and `capture.lisp` (`capture-state` of a given sim, `with-capture-game`, `fly-screenshots` and `fly-main` for `scripts/fly`); the simulation files: `math.lisp`, `units.lisp`, `bodies.lisp`, `orbits.lisp` (also the Lambert solver `lambert-into!` and `j2-mean-semi-major-axis`), `physics.lisp` (also the objects: `object-def`, `object`, rails segments, `state-rails` at an integrated state's mean motion, attitude modes), `ship.lisp` (the Mule's mass, engine and RCS; the stack with a load, collision boxes, the range finder `grapple-range-into!`), `places.lisp` (`*objects*`, the twelve objects in Earth orbit, and `*places*`, the five autopilot presets; the rendezvous stop points, the terminal frame, the fixture choice), `controls.lisp` (the flight computer, holds 1–6 and R, the target holds 7–0 with their transported roll, the burn plan `planned-burn` and `burn-plan` with a rendezvous's `rendezvous-part`, the terminal law `terminal-controls!`, the mixer, `flight-step!`), `clock.lisp` (the sim, its step with the burn plan's executor `plan-step-controls!` and the autopilot's rules `plan-step-checks!` (completion, aborts, the attitude cut), a rendezvous's `:terminal` and `:station` by `terminal-step!` (on station, its aborts, ×4), the clock with `autopilot-engage!` and `autopilot-disengage!`, the planning barrier and the frame's budget split, checkpoints), `hazards.lisp` (crashes, removals, and contact with promoted objects: the step's start in a scratch, broad and narrow phase, bounce redone on the grid, death `:contact`; the grapple's tip, capture into the stack or onto a berth, the latched load and the berthed hold), `prediction.lisp` (the copy flying the plan, shots `prediction-shoot!`, the target's nodes, closest approach and relative nodes, a shot's clearance window `prediction-clearance!`, the work budget), `autopilot.lisp` (burns: `finite-burn`, `burn-attitude`, `slew-estimate`, the lead time and reserve; the coast shot and `autopilot-first-guess` for circularise, set apoapsis or periapsis, Hohmann and plane changes; the planning job `autopilot-job`, its shots, goal measures and refusals, `autopilot-plan-now`; the rendezvous: aim points, the search `rendezvous-search-now`, the job `:rendezvous` with its lead, the re-timing to the promoted target, shooting to the aim point, path check and fallback, `rendezvous-plan-aim-point`; the preset menu `autopilot-model` in `clock-autopilot` with its RDV rows, `autopilot-key!`, `autopilot-request!` and `autopilot-confirm!` for scripts, `autopilot-menu-lines`, `autopilot-plan-summary`), `flights.lisp` (the flight scripts: `read-flight`, the safe reader and step parser; the start states `(:start :state …)`, `stated-sim` and `flight-start-sim`; `run-flight`, headless, with `flight-step-hook` and the record, `flight-record-lines`; the autopilot steps (`(:autopilot …)` engaged at the snapshot + 60 s), labelled burns, `:autopilot-done` and the record's expectations with the clearance `flight-engage` keeps; the `:approach` pilot for the last 100 m, `approach-tick!`; live `fly`, `flight-status`, `flight-stop`); `assets.lisp` (the assets root, image decoding and the 1/8 copy, the star table); the rendering files: `gpu.lisp` (HAL helpers, the renderer), `camera.lisp` (the camera, the one projection, the outside view, the map view), `stars.lisp` (the HYG stars at infinity, their colours), `impostors.lisp` (the Sun, the Moon, the Blue Marble Earth and its halo as ray-traced sphere impostors), `post.lisp` (HDR, bloom, tone map, exposure), `overlay.lisp` (the 5×7 glyph table, `label-at!`, the map's marker shapes and label placement, the minimal readout: `readout-lines` and `readout-vertices!`, the message line: `message-line-lines`, the autopilot panel: `autopilot-panel-lines` and `autopilot-panel-origin`, the LOST panel: `lost-panel-lines`, the overlay pipeline), `text.lisp` (Slug text on planes: one atlas of three fonts, grid and shaped runs, the text batch, the `:cab-text` draw), `cockpit.lisp` (the DIY cab: its model, materials and light, the openings, the `:cab-mesh` and `:cab-instanced` pipelines' resources; the shell, fittings and LED strips so far), `cockpit-screens.lisp` (after `render.lisp`: the screens' lines as pure functions, `screen-lines` for ORBIT and SHIP so far, the line batch's writes, the 10 Hz rebuild and `cockpit-state`, `*cockpit-instruments*`), `cockpit-devices.lisp` (the lamps with `lamp-condition`, MSTR, the PROP and hull gauges with their lagged needles, the throttle lever), `cockpit-buttons.lisp` (the 18 button lenses with their lighting, the clickables, `cockpit-pick`, `cockpit-click!`, the hover outline), `cockpit-autopilot.lisp` (the AUTOPILOT screen's orbit plot: `autopilot-plot-segments!`), `cockpit-attitude.lisp` (the ATTITUDE plot: `attitude-plot-point`, the markers, the hold's cross, the rate bars, `attitude-view-update!`), `cockpit-grapple.lisp` (the grapple camera on the TARGET screen: `grapple-camera-update!`, the pin's projection, TARGET's armed lines on bands, the crosshair and the pin's ◆; the pass itself is `render.lisp`'s `encode-grapple-pass`); `render.lisp` (the render state with its objects and grapple, the floodlight in an object's axes and `range-finder-spot`, the frame's passes, the objects' draws: `object-uniforms!`, `object-glows!`, `object-draw-kind`, `encode-objects`); `meshes.lisp` (the grapple's claws, floodlight and range-finder lights (`ship-claw-instances!`, `ship-glows!`, `ship-spot-glow!`); the Mule's generated meshes, instances, decal and bounding radius, and its draws: hull, instances, engine, navigation lights, strobe and engine glow; the ship builders, the fixture mesh and the places' models with their scenery ships and lights, `object-model`, the objects' buffers and slots: `object-gpu`, `object-slot`); `ribbons.lisp` (the map: the predicted trajectory `trajectory-ribbon-points!` (merged runs and Hermite splits within 0.25 px) in place of the osculating orbit, which stays its fallback; the osculating orbit as points split by pixel error, per-segment instances, the `:ribbon` pipeline drawing it 2 px wide, red below the crash radius; `map-markers`: the ship, AP, PE, the impact, the plan's burns and bodies below 6 px, with their labels and visibility; the plan's dashed path, goal orbit and kept grey from `autopilot-map-plan`); `input.lisp` (the input state, `handle-event`, `input-frame!`: held keys and taps to `controls` once per frame, none while the autopilot is engaged; the autopilot's keys); `docs/architecture.md` "Files in load order" lists every file, milestone by milestone, and what each holds.
- `tests/`: Parachute tests, one file per `src/` file it checks (`package.lisp` first), the system `luv-in-space/tests`; `slow-flights.lisp`, the system `luv-in-space/slow-tests` (`decision 123`); `docs/architecture.md` "Testing" gives each test and its bound.
- `assets/`: runtime data, one directory per kind with its `SOURCE.md` (`assets/earth/blue-marble-5400.jpg`, `black-marble-5400.jpg` and `clouds-5400.jpg` (`scripts/convert-earth`); `assets/stars/hyg-6.5.sexp`, the HYG stars ≤ 6.5 mag, under CC BY-SA 4.0 with its `LICENSE`; `assets/fonts/`, Monaspace Neon and Iosevka Aile under the SIL OFL 1.1 with their licences); `CREDITS.md` at the root; the package installs `assets/` and the launcher sets `LUV_IN_SPACE_ASSETS` (`docs/architecture.md` "Assets").
- `flights/`: the flight scripts `burn`, `raise-orbit`, `deorbit`; `approach-tern`, `approach-k-17`, `rendezvous-fulmar-phasing`, `rendezvous-skua-yard`, `skua-yard-apogee-approach`; `cockpit-k-17`, the rendezvous and capture of K-17 in the cockpit view; `preset-tern-station`, `preset-fulmar-depot`, `preset-k-17`, `preset-highport`, `preset-skua-yard`, the presets by autopilot from the start state (`<name>.sexp`, read by `read-flight`, flown by `run-flight "<name>"`); the package installs them beside `assets/` (`docs/architecture.md` "Scripts by milestone").
- `scripts/`: `test`, `test-slow`, `screenshot`, `fly`, `convert-earth`, `convert-stars` (with `convert-stars.lisp`), the build entry (`build.lisp`), `build-sly-client` and `sly-client.lisp` (the `./sly` client); `./sly` and `sly-server.lisp` at the root.
- `docs/`: the source of truth for features after `milestone 1`; cite their sections when implementing.
  - `luv-capabilities.md`: what luv offers, measured, and the gaps with their workarounds here.
  - `luv-wishlist.md`: what this repo builds that would better live in luv.
  - `architecture.md`: the dependency on luv, module layout, units and frames, integrator and time warp, tests and flight scripts.
  - `design.md`: the game for implementers; "Scope" → "Feature list" assigns every feature to `milestone 2` to `milestone 6` or cuts it.
  - `story.md`: setting, characters, the quest line, every radio text; "Names and terms" is the one list of names.
